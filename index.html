Trade responsibly

Trading involves risk. There are no guaranteed profits, and you can lose money.

Important safety reminders
  • Practise on demo before considering a real account.
  • Never trade money you cannot afford to lose.
  • Avoid borrowing money to trade or using high leverage.
  • Use risk controls and understand every trade before placing it.
  • Be careful of anyone promising guaranteed profits or asking for your password.
